# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work closely with Product Managers to turn requirements into working features
- Partner with Technical Leads for architecture decisions and implementation trade-offs
- Coordinate with QA/Testing Lead on quality gates and test coverage
- Provide status updates to Project Managers on execution progress and blockers

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Align with Project Managers on scope, timeline, and resourcing
- Work with UX/Design Lead on user needs, experience, and validation
- Partner with Technical Leads to assess feasibility and trade-offs
- Share priorities and success metrics with Stakeholders/Sponsors and QA teams

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Coordinate across Developers, Product Managers, and Technical Leads to keep work moving
- Escalate issues to Stakeholders/Sponsors when business decisions or approvals are needed
- Work with Security Lead and QA/Testing Lead on release readiness and risk tracking
- Provide organizational context and dependency visibility to the broader delivery team

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define the quality strategy, test planning, and release readiness checks. They help ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and that risk is reduced before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and quality assurance strategy
- Define and validate acceptance criteria with Product Managers and delivery teams
- Coordinate manual testing and automated test execution
- Identify quality issues, regression risks, and release blockers
- Participate in release readiness reviews and feature sign-off
- Document known issues and quality limitations

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality products that meet user and business expectations
- Catch defects early in the delivery lifecycle
- Maintain reliable coverage and confidence in releases

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with Product and Engineering teams
- Daily standup updates on coverage, blockers, and defects
- Quality gate discussions before release

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Developers to validate feature behavior and prevent regressions
- Works with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria are measurable and testable
- Shares risk and defect status with Project Managers and Stakeholders/Sponsors
- Coordinates with Security Lead on security-related testing and compliance checks

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, decision authority, and resource alignment. They validate that projects deliver meaningful value and remove organizational barriers to success.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities, success metrics, and strategic alignment
- Make go/no-go decisions at project milestones
- Approve trade-offs in scope, budget, or timeline
- Review project progress and key risks
- Remove organizational barriers and unblock dependencies
- Validate business outcomes after launch

### Goals
- Ensure initiatives align with business strategy
- Maximize return on investment and customer impact
- Maintain executive visibility and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Milestone reviews and decision gate discussions
- Escalation of major risks or blockers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm priorities and business outcomes
- Relies on Project Managers for status and risk reporting
- Escalates major issues to executive or cross-functional stakeholders as needed
- Collaborates with Technical and Security Leads on strategic decisions that affect delivery risk

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, architectural oversight, and implementation guidance. They help ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with engineering standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve system design and technical architecture
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Guide implementation choices, patterns, and trade-offs
- Mentor team members and reinforce engineering standards
- Coordinate with platform, infrastructure, and partner teams
- Support integration and dependency management across systems

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce long-term technical debt and avoid fragile implementations
- Enable efficient delivery without sacrificing quality or stability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review guidance and engineering standards alignment
- Dependency and integration planning across teams

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with Developers to guide implementation quality and architecture decisions
- Aligns with Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and delivery constraints
- Coordinates with Project Managers to surface technical blockers and dependencies
- Partners with Security Lead to ensure secure-by-design decisions and risk mitigation

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads define the user experience direction, validate usability, and help ensure solutions are intuitive, accessible, and valuable to end users.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate design decisions with users and stakeholders
- Ensure accessibility and design system consistency
- Review implementation for design fidelity and usability
- Identify design risks and iterate based on feedback

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and user-centered experiences
- Validate product-market fit through user feedback
- Reduce usability issues before release

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and product discovery sessions
- User feedback and usability testing summaries
- Collaboration with engineering on implementation fidelity

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to align user outcomes with business goals
- Works with Developers to ensure designs are implemented correctly
- Shares insights with Project Managers on timeline and dependency impacts
- Collaborates with QA/Testing Lead on usability validation and acceptance criteria

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure that security requirements are considered throughout the project lifecycle and that risks are addressed before release.

### Responsibilities
- Assess security requirements and compliance obligations
- Perform threat analysis and risk reviews
- Review architecture and implementation for security vulnerabilities
- Coordinate security scanning, testing, and remediation planning
- Support incident response and recovery activities
- Maintain security-related documentation and runbooks

### Goals
- Build security into solutions from the start
- Reduce compliance and vulnerability risks
- Enable safe, trusted releases

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during planning and design
- Risk assessments and remediation plans
- Security escalation and incident coordination

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Technical Leads and Developers to reduce architectural and code-level risks
- Partners with Project Managers on release readiness and risk tracking
- Supports Product Managers and Stakeholders/Sponsors with security trade-offs and compliance implications
- Coordinates with QA/Testing Lead on security validation and release criteria
